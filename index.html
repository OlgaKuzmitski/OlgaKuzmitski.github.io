Here's who I am & what I do

Welcome to my professional nursing portfolio!

My name is Olga Kuzmitski, and I am a graduate of William Paterson University with a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN). I have over 10 years of nursing experience in various healthcare settings, including pediatric home health care, telemetry, step-down nursing, and nursing leadership.

My nursing journey began as a Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN). Through continued education, dedication, and professional growth, I became a Registered Nurse (RN) and later advanced into leadership roles. One of my most valuable experiences was working as a Clinical Manager in pediatric home health care in New Jersey, where I was responsible for coordinating patient care, supporting nursing staff, and working closely with patients and their families.

I also gained valuable hospital experience at Hackensack Meridian Health in New Jersey, where I cared for patients with complex medical conditions. These experiences strengthened my clinical judgment, critical thinking, and ability to work effectively in challenging situations.

Currently, I work as a Clinical Nurse Coordinator (CNC) on the Step-Down Neuro unit at HCA Florida Aventura Hospital. In this role, I coordinate daily nursing activities, support staff, assist with patient care decisions, and promote teamwork, accountability, and patient safety.

Throughout my career, I have learned that effective nursing leadership requires more than clinical knowledge. It requires compassion, strong communication, teamwork, and the ability to support others while maintaining high standards of patient care.

My professional goal is to continue advancing in nursing leadership and management, where I can use my clinical experience and leadership skills to make a positive difference for patients, nurses, and the healthcare team.

This portfolio highlights my professional experience, education, accomplishments, and continued commitment to nursing excellence.
